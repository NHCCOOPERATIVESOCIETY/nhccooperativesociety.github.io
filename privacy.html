Privacy Policy

NHC Co-operative Society System
Operated by the NHC Co-operative Society, Minna, Nigeria.
Last updated: October 2026

1. About this app

The NHC Co-operative Society System is the online member portal of the NHC Co-operative Society. It lets registered members and society officials manage membership records, monthly contributions, applications for financial assistance, loan repayments, announcements and society elections. The app is for members and officials of the society only.

2. Information we collect

From Google Sign-In: when you sign in with your Google account, we receive your name and email address. We use the "openid", "email" and "profile" permissions only. We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar or any other data in your Google account.

From your membership with the society: information you or the society officials give us when you register and use the system, namely your member ID, phone number, passport photograph, contribution records, assistance requests and repayment records, guarantor details, and your votes in society elections where applicable.

Security information: a password hash if you set a portal password (we never store your actual password), and session records that keep you signed in on your device.

3. How we use your information

  • To confirm that you are a registered, active member before letting you in.
  • To show you your own account, contributions and repayments.
  • To process membership registration, assistance applications, guarantor confirmations and official approvals.
  • To send you society notices and announcements.
  • To keep the society's financial records accurate and to protect the system from misuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

4. Who can see your information

You can see your own records. Authorised society officials (such as the Secretary, Financial Secretary, Chairman, Treasurer and PRO) can see the records they need to do their duties. Where you act as a guarantor, the applicant and the approving officials can see your name and decision. We do not share your information with any third party except where the law requires us to.

5. Where your information is stored

Records are stored in the society's own Google Sheets and Google Drive, and the system runs on Google Apps Script. Your data is therefore held on Google's infrastructure under the society's account. The sign-in page is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not store any member data.

6. Data security

Access to the records is limited to the society's authorised accounts. Members are admitted only if they are active on the membership register, and deactivated members are signed out. Sessions end automatically after a period of inactivity, and a password lock protects the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. How long we keep your information

We keep membership and financial records for as long as you are a member and for the period the society needs them for its accounting and audit purposes. Session records expire automatically. When a membership ends, the member is deactivated and can no longer sign in.

8. Your choices and rights

You may ask the society to show you the information held about you, to correct it, or to delete it where the society is not required to keep it. You can also remove this app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To make a request, contact the Secretary at the address below.

9. Google user data

Our use of information received from Google is limited to identifying you and confirming your membership, as described above. We do not transfer it to others, use it for advertising, or allow people to read it except as needed to run the society's services, for security, or to comply with the law.

10. Children

This app is for adult members of the society and is not intended for children.

11. Changes to this policy
