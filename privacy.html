Privacy Policy

The NHC Cooperative Society System uses Google Sign-In only to confirm the email address of a registered member. We receive your name and email address from Google and use them only to identify you inside the cooperative's portal.

Member records (contributions, assistance requests and repayments) are stored in the cooperative's own Google Sheet and are visible only to authorised society officials. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

To ask for a correction or removal of your details, contact the Secretary of the society.
