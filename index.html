NHC Co-operative Society System

The NHC Co-operative Society System is the online member portal of the NHC Co-operative Society. It is used only by members and officials of the society.

What the app does

  • Member registration and membership records
  • Monthly contributions and statements of account
  • Applications for financial assistance, guarantor confirmation and official approvals
  • Repayment schedules, announcements and society elections

Why we use Google Sign-In

Members sign in with their Google account so the system can confirm their email address and check that they are registered members. We use only your name and email address, and nothing else from your Google account.
