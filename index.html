About the Portal

The NHC Co-operative Society System is a private management utility designed for members and officials of the society. This secure portal allows authorized users to track their financial contributions, view dividend distributions, manage loan balances, and process guarantor approvals.

Our application utilizes Google OAuth login strictly as a secure identity verification mechanism to match incoming sessions with pre-registered society email profiles.
